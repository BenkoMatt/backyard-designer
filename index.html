How to Use Backyard Designer 3DBasic

View Keyboard Shortcuts — press ? or F1 anytime

Getting Started

  • Click an item from the left panel to add it to your yard
  • Click an object in the 3D view to select it
  • Click and drag an object to reposition it
  • Use the right panel to adjust size, color, and other properties

Basic vs Advanced Mode

  • Basic mode (default) shows essential tools: Terrain sculpting, Sun & Shadow, and core object placement
  • Advanced mode reveals all features: Underground excavation, Terrain Analysis, Pro Tools, Measure, Atmosphere, Export, Gallery, Seasonal Planning, and more
  • Toggle between modes using the Basic/Advanced buttons in the top bar, or press M
  • Your mode choice is saved and will persist across sessions
  • Most keyboard shortcuts work in both modes — the Command Palette (Ctrl+K) shows only relevant commands in Basic mode

Camera Controls

  • 3D View: Left-click + drag to orbit • Scroll to zoom • Right-click + drag to pan
  • Bird's-eye (2D): Top-down view for precise placement • Scroll to zoom • Drag to pan
  • Use the zoom buttons (bottom-right) or the reset button to reframe

Saving & Sharing

  • Save downloads your design as a JSON file you can reload later
  • Your design auto-saves to your browser as you work
  • Screenshot captures a PNG image of your current view
  • Share opens the Share modal where you can export your design as JSON, generate a shareable link, or create a QR code
  • Gallery (Advanced mode) saves screenshots to an in-app gallery — capture multiple views and browse them anytime
  • Social Card (Advanced mode) generates a beautiful shareable card image with your design title and a preview screenshot

Terrain & Measuring

  • Terrain: Click the Terrain button (or press T; X toggles), then click-drag the ground to sculpt. Seven brush modes — Raise, Lower, Smooth, Erode, Dig, Fill, Flatten — via keys 1–7; [ / ] changes brush size (1–30 ft)
  • Adjust brush size and strength in the panel that appears. Use Precision Mode for fine adjustments (brush limited to 10 ft, strength to 0.2)
  • Objects automatically follow the terrain height when you move them
  • Tape Measure: Click two points to measure the distance between them
  • Excavate: Click the Excavate button to open the underground view — cut away terrain, make ground transparent, toggle wireframe, or see which objects are buried
  • Cross-Section: In the Excavate panel, click Cross-Section then click two points on the ground to see a 2D elevation profile
  • Scale Bar: The bar at bottom-left shows a reference distance in feet
  • In Bird's-eye view, grid labels show foot measurements along the edges

Keyboard Shortcuts

Press ? or F1 (or click ⌨ Shortcuts in the top bar) for the complete Keyboard Shortcuts guide — every key for terrain, camera, editing, files, and modes, organized by category.

  • Ctrl+K — Command Palette (search any feature)
  • Ctrl+Z — Undo • Ctrl+Y / Ctrl+Shift+Z — Redo • Ctrl+D — Duplicate • Ctrl+A — Select All (Advanced)
  • Alt+Tab — Cycle Through Objects • Shift+Alt+Tab — Reverse
  • Escape — Deselect / Close Panels
  • Autosave: Your work is saved automatically about every 30 seconds and when you leave the page. If the browser closes before you save, a banner offers to restore your unsaved changes next time.

Advanced Features

  • Walk Mode: Explore in first person — WASD or arrows to move (Shift = faster), mouse to look, Escape to exit
  • Cost Estimator: Click the Cost button to see a cost breakdown of all objects in your design. Estimates are based on typical material costs.
  • Templates: Open the Templates modal to browse pre-made garden designs. Click a template to load it into your yard as a starting point.
  • Layers: Click the Layers button to toggle visibility of different object categories. Hide plants, structures, or terrain elements independently.
  • Command Palette (Ctrl+K): Quick access to any feature — search by name and press Enter.
  • Seasons: Click the Season button to preview how your yard looks in spring, summer, autumn, or winter — see snow coverage and fall foliage.
  • Timelapse: Open the Timelapse modal to generate an animated build sequence of your design, showing objects being placed one by one. Play the animation and export frames.
  • Social Card: Generate a polished shareable card image with your design title and a preview screenshot — perfect for sharing on social media.
  • Multi-Select: Hold Shift or Ctrl while clicking objects to select multiple. Use the batch bar to delete all or delete by type.
  • Right-Click Context Menu: Right-click any object for quick Duplicate, Rotate, Delete, and Properties actions.
  • Underground Carving: Click Excavate (bottom-left) to open the underground view — cutaway, transparency, wireframe, buried objects. Cross-Section shows a 2D profile between two points.
  • Underground View: After digging (Dig brush, key 5, or Excavate), open the Underground view to see geological layers — topsoil, subsoil, clay, bedrock — in the hole walls
  • Terrain Dock Sections: Grid Level & Depth, Carving, and Presets & Tools collapse behind accordion headers — click to expand
  • Pro Terrain Tools: Pro Tools dock has the pool excavation wizard, precision grading, and advanced sculpting

Safety Reminders

  • Pool barriers must be at least 48" high with self-closing, self-latching gates
  • Call MISS DIG 811 (8-1-1 or 800-482-3173) before any excavation — it's Michigan law
  • Keep fire pits at least 25 feet from structures and property lines
  • Retaining walls over 4 feet need engineering — consult a professional
  • Grade soil away from your house: 6" drop over 10 feet (5% slope)

Accessibility Tips

  • All buttons and controls are keyboard accessible — use Tab to move through them
  • Object library items can be added by focusing and pressing Enter or Space
  • Status messages (save success, cost updates) are announced to screen readers automatically
  • If you prefer reduced motion, enable "Reduce motion" in your system settings — animations will be minimized
