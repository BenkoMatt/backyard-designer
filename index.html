How to Use Backyard Designer 3D

Getting Started

  • Click an item from the left panel to add it to your yard
  • Click an object in the 3D view to select it
  • Drag the Move icon (appears above selected objects) to reposition
  • Use the right panel to adjust size, color, and other properties

Camera Controls

  • 3D View: Left-click + drag to orbit • Scroll to zoom • Right-click + drag to pan
  • Bird's-eye (2D): Top-down view for precise placement • Scroll to zoom • Drag to pan
  • Use the zoom buttons (bottom-right) or the reset button to reframe

Saving & Sharing

  • Save downloads your design as a file you can reload later
  • Your design auto-saves to your browser as you work
  • Screenshot captures a PNG image of your current view

Terrain & Measuring

  • Terrain: Click the Terrain button, then click-drag on the ground to raise/lower/smooth the surface to match slopes and grade changes
  • Adjust brush size and strength in the panel that appears
  • Objects automatically follow the terrain height when you move them
  • Tape Measure: Click two points to measure the distance between them
  • Scale Bar: The bar at bottom-left shows a reference distance in feet
  • In Bird's-eye view, grid labels show foot measurements along the edges

Safety Reminders

  • Pool barriers must be at least 48" high with self-closing, self-latching gates
  • Call MISS DIG 811 (8-1-1 or 800-482-3173) before any excavation — it's Michigan law
  • Keep fire pits at least 25 feet from structures and property lines
  • Retaining walls over 4 feet need engineering — consult a professional
  • Grade soil away from your house: 6" drop over 10 feet (5% slope)
