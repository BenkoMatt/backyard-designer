**1) Overlapping / clipped elements**
- No hard overlaps or clipping found. Everything stays inside the 1280×800 frame, the floating SCULPT/BUILD/VIEW panel clears the status bar, and the bottom button row clears the zoom controls. Minor note: the compass sits ~8px from the "Take Tour" button — very tight, at risk on narrower screens.

**2) 5-second comprehension — borderline yes**
- Works in the user's favor: clear product name, an obvious "Take Tour" call-to-action, and a well-labeled object library (icons + descriptions) that suggests "pick an item, place it in the yard."
- Works against it: the canvas is a bare empty grid with no hint text (click-to-place vs. drag is unknown), and the app loads in **Advanced** mode showing sculpting tools a first-time user doesn't need. "Basic" would be a better default. A new user will likely understand it's a design tool but may hesitate on the first action.

**3) Confusing / ambiguous / broken-looking**
- **"Innovate"** button (bottom row) — almost certainly a typo for "Irrigate"; as written it's meaningless next to Tape Measure/Terrain/Excavate.
- **Duplicate tools**: "Terrain," "Analyze," and "Measure/Tape Measure" appear in *both* the left floating panel and the bottom action bar — unclear which is canonical and whether they do the same thing.
- **"FPS: —"** in the status bar looks unfinished/placeholder.
- The left floating panel has no header, anchor, or close control — it just hovers over the canvas.
- "Lawn Area" is grouped under **Patios & Paths** — miscategorized.
- Minor: "Brush: 8 ft" shown while active tool is Select.

**Verdict: not CLEAN** — fix "Innovate," the duplicated toolbars, and default to Basic for new users.
